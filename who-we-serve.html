Who We Serve

Replacing out-of-state corporate laboratory reliance with highly responsive local precision. Explore how our processing architecture integrates directly with your operations.

01 // CLINICAL

Physician Practices & Specialty Clinics

Many physician practices and specialty clinics depend on national laboratory networks where specimens travel across multiple states before processing begins. Additional transport stages, fragmented communication channels, and external routing dependencies can create delays that affect scheduling, treatment planning, and day-to-day clinical workflow coordination.

AccessPoint Dx keeps specimen processing and laboratory communication within Minnesota, creating a direct operational connection between your practice and our Twin Cities laboratory infrastructure. Through integrated reporting pathways and electronic health record connectivity, diagnostic information flows directly into your existing systems rather than requiring staff to navigate multiple external portals.

The result is a streamlined diagnostic workflow designed to improve visibility, reduce administrative friction, and support faster clinical decision-making.

02 // RESPONSIVE

Long-Term Care & Hospice Communities

Long-term care and hospice organizations operate in environments where continuity, responsiveness, and coordinated care are essential. Clinical teams frequently manage high-acuity populations while balancing evolving treatment needs, staffing considerations, and daily operational schedules.

AccessPoint Dx supports these environments through localized specimen routing and collection workflows structured around the operational rhythm of each facility rather than rigid national pickup windows. Predictable statewide turnaround pathways and accelerated reporting options for qualifying metrics help clinical teams obtain information quickly enough to support timely care decisions.

Our infrastructure is designed to reduce operational disruption and provide the diagnostic visibility necessary to maintain consistent resident-centered care.

03 // STRATEGIC

Occupational Health & Workforce Compliance

Workplace testing programs require operational efficiency, clear documentation standards, and workflows that support both organizational safety priorities and regulatory compliance obligations. Delays in collection, off-site travel requirements, and fragmented reporting systems can create unnecessary administrative burden for employers and employees alike.

AccessPoint Dx supports modern workforce compliance programs through localized collection workflows and non-invasive oral fluid testing pathways aligned with current regulatory frameworks. Secure on-site collections can reduce employee travel requirements while automated reporting workflows feed directly into organizational tracking systems.

By connecting laboratory data with operational reporting systems, organizations gain a more efficient compliance infrastructure with reduced administrative complexity and stronger process visibility.

04 // CIVIC

Municipal & Public Safety Agencies

Public safety agencies operate within environments where personnel availability and operational readiness directly affect community response capabilities. Traditional collection workflows can require officers and personnel to leave active duty assignments for extended periods while traveling, waiting, and completing documentation procedures.

Recent developments supporting oral fluid testing create opportunities for more efficient collection pathways and reduced personnel downtime. AccessPoint Dx provides localized collection infrastructure, auditable chain-of-custody systems, and secure reporting workflows designed around the operational realities of municipal organizations.

By reducing unnecessary administrative and logistical delays, agencies can preserve personnel availability, improve workflow efficiency, and maintain stronger continuity of active field operations.
