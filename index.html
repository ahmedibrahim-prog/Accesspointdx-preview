Why Local Processing Matters

Closer specimens mean faster clinical decisions.

Regional processing reduces transport complexity and supports faster reporting pathways for providers and patients. Keeping your testing pipeline close to your clinic reduces cross-country transit bottlenecks and gives you a direct line to our laboratory staff.
